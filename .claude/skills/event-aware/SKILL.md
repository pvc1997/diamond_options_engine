Event-aware trading:

1. Key Indian market events:
   - RBI monetary policy (bi-monthly)
   - Union Budget (February)
   - Company earnings (quarterly)
   - US Fed decisions (affect global sentiment)
   - General elections
   - FII/DII flow reversals

2. Pre-event strategy:
   - IV typically rises 2-5 days before known events
   - Buying options before event = paying inflated IV
   - Selling premium before event = high premium but directional risk
   - Best: sell premium AFTER the event (IV crush)

3. Post-event IV crush:
   - IV drops 20-50% after event passes
   - Short straddles/strangles entered after announcement benefit
   - Long options lose value even if direction is right (vega drag)

4. Event playbook:
   - Earnings: Short straddle after results, not before
   - RBI: Short strangle if expected to be non-event
   - Budget: Stay flat or hedge — too unpredictable
   - Elections: Reduce all positions, increase hedges
